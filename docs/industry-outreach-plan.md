# CRE Calculators: Industry Outreach Plan

This plan focuses on earning legitimate, high-quality backlinks and traffic from established real estate education, brokerage, and syndication platforms.

## 1. Core Rule & Compliance
- **No Paid Links:** Do not buy backlinks, sponsor posts explicitly for links, or participate in private blog networks (PBNs). This violates Google's Webmaster Guidelines and risks manual penalties.
- **Value-First Approach:** We offer free, high-quality tools (especially the Deal Analyzer and Cash-on-Cash Calculator) that make brokers, educators, and syndicators look good when they share them with their audience.

## 2. Target Personas & Outreach Angles

### A. Real Estate Educators & Course Creators
- **Who:** YouTubers, podcasters, and bloggers teaching multifamily investing (e.g., BiggerPockets contributors, independent CRE mentors).
- **The Pitch:** "I noticed you teach underwriting in your recent [Video/Article]. We built a free, no-signup Deal Analyzer that calculates Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash, and DSCR simultaneously. It might be a great free resource to link to for your students/viewers who want to run the numbers themselves."
- **Assets:** The 24-unit multifamily "Worked Example" on our pages perfectly mirrors the case studies they teach.

### B. Boutique Commercial Brokerages
- **Who:** Regional commercial real estate brokers who publish market reports, guides for buyers, or newsletters.
- **The Pitch:** "Brokers often spend too much time explaining basic math to new investors. You can link to our Cash-on-Cash or NOI calculators on your 'Resources' page so your buyers can quickly pre-qualify their own deals before bringing them to you."

### C. Real Estate Syndicators & Sponsors
- **Who:** General Partners (GPs) who raise capital from Limited Partners (LPs).
- **The Pitch:** "Educated LPs are easier to raise from. Feel free to use our Cash-on-Cash vs IRR comparison guide and Deal Analyzer in your investor education newsletters to help your LPs understand how you underwrite."

## 3. Recommended Outreach Execution

1. **Prospecting:** Build a list of 50–100 targets using search queries like:
   - `inurl:resources "commercial real estate"`
   - `"how to calculate cash on cash return" "real estate" -crecalculators.com`
   - `site:youtube.com "multifamily underwriting"`
2. **Contact:** Use email or LinkedIn direct messages. Keep it to 3 sentences maximum.
3. **Follow-up:** 1 polite follow-up after 4-5 days. If no response, move on.

## 4. Why This Works
The recent updates to our calculators—adding the interconnected 24-unit example, comparison tables, and clarifying positive/negative leverage—transform the pages from "just code" into actual educational resources. They solve a tangible pain point for industry professionals (educating their audience) without them having to build the software themselves.
